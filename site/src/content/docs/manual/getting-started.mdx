This page takes you from the download to the first question you hear. It takes about 5 minutes on a 50 Mbps connection, plus the time to download Cloudy Reader. The [Onboarding guide](/manual/onboarding/) is the short version of these steps.

## Check your Mac

<Steps>

1. Choose **Apple menu > About This Mac**.
2. Read the chip. A Mac with Apple silicon shows an Apple chip, such as Apple M2. A Mac that shows an Intel processor is an Intel-based Mac.
3. Read the macOS version. It must be REL-MACOS-FLOOR or later.
4. Make sure the Mac is online and has a working sound output.
5. Make sure at least REL-DISK-TOTAL of disk space is free. **System Settings > General > Storage** shows the free space.

</Steps>

The chip is an Apple chip, macOS is REL-MACOS-FLOOR or later, and REL-DISK-TOTAL is free.

An Intel-based Mac is not supported, and there is no workaround. If macOS is older than REL-MACOS-FLOOR, choose **System Settings > General > Software Update** and install any update to REL-MACOS-FLOOR or later. If no such update appears, the Mac is not supported. See [Your Mac is not supported](/manual/troubleshooting/#your-mac-is-not-supported). If the disk is too full, free some space before you go on.

## Download and install

<Steps>

1. Open [Download Cloudy Reader](/download/) and download the zip (REL-ZIP-SIZE).
2. Optional: check the download with the checksum command on that page. It runs in Terminal and confirms that the file is complete and unchanged.
3. Find the zip in the **Downloads** folder in Finder and double-click it to unzip it. If your browser already unzipped it, skip this step.
4. Drag **Cloudy Reader** to the **Applications** folder. Do this before you open the app.

</Steps>

Cloudy Reader is in the **Applications** folder. Delete the zip to free REL-ZIP-SIZE, or keep it to install the app on another Mac.

If the checksum does not match, delete the zip and download it again. See [The checksum does not match](/manual/troubleshooting/#the-checksum-does-not-match). If the mismatch repeats, follow [Report a security issue](/manual/get-help/#report-a-security-issue).

To build the app from source instead, see [Build from source](/developer/building/).

## Open the app

The app is signed ad hoc and is not notarized, so macOS asks you to approve the first launch once. Approve only an app that comes from a source you trust. The dialog wording differs between macOS versions.

<Steps>

1. Open **Applications** and double-click Cloudy Reader.
2. macOS says it cannot verify the app. Close the warning dialog, and do not move the app to the Trash. This message is expected: see [macOS cannot verify the app](/manual/troubleshooting/#macos-cannot-verify-the-app).
3. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
4. Scroll to **Security** and find the message about Cloudy Reader.
5. Click **Open Anyway**. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. If you use a standard account, an administrator must enter a name and password instead.
6. Open Cloudy Reader again. A second dialog appears: click **Open**.

</Steps>

[Approve the app](/manual/tasks/first-open/#approve-the-app) has the full procedure.

The Cloudy Reader window opens.

If there is no **Open Anyway** button, it shows only after a blocked attempt: [open the app again to bring it back](/manual/tasks/first-open/#if-open-anyway-is-missing). For other causes, see [Open Anyway is missing](/manual/troubleshooting/#open-anyway-is-missing). If macOS says the app is damaged, see [macOS says the app is damaged](/manual/troubleshooting/#macos-says-the-app-is-damaged). On a school or work Mac, ask the administrator.

## Let the voices download

Cloudy Reader downloads its voices at the first launch, about REL-DOWNLOAD-SIZE in all.

<Steps>

1. Keep the window open and the Mac online. Closing the window stops the download.
2. Read the status bar at the bottom of the window. It shows "Loading voices…" briefly, then "No voice is installed." On the right, it shows "Downloading voices… _N_%" and a progress bar.
3. Look at the **Voice** pop-up menu below the question box and the **Speak** button in the row of buttons. Both are dimmed.
4. Wait for the first voice to arrive. It usually takes under a minute.

</Steps>

<Figure
  src="window-first-launch-light.svg"
  dark="window-first-launch-dark.svg"
  alt="The Cloudy Reader window at first launch: the Voice menu reads No voice installed, Speak is dimmed, and the status bar says No voice is installed. beside Downloading voices… 4% and a progress bar."
  caption="The status bar shows the warning on the left and the download progress on the right."
  frame="window"
/>

The **Voice** menu shows a voice, and **Speak** is no longer dimmed. You can go on to the next section now. The download continues in the background.

When the download finishes, the progress label and bar leave the status bar, and the other voices appear in the **Voice** menu. The app then selects the first voice in the menu, unless you chose a voice yourself. After that, reading needs no network. [Manage voices and storage](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/) shows how to check the download.

Check the **Voice** menu, not the percentage. A failed file counts as finished, so the percentage can rise while no voice arrives. If the label and bar leave the status bar and the menu is still empty, the download failed, and the status bar keeps "No voice is installed." with no other message.

To retry a failed download, connect the Mac to the internet, quit the app with Command-Q, and open it again. The download starts only at launch. See [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed) and [The voice download does not progress](/manual/troubleshooting/#the-voice-download-does-not-progress).

If some voices could not be downloaded, a warning can appear at the left of the status bar when no other message is showing. Quit the app and open it again to retry. See [Some voices could not be downloaded](/manual/troubleshooting/#some-voices-could-not-be-downloaded).

## Hear a sample question

Copy this toss-up. It has a chemical formula, so you hear how the app says one. The first reading after the app opens can take several seconds longer, because the app loads its name-detection model once.

```text wrap
TOSS-UP
1) CHEMISTRY Multiple Choice Which gas forms when sodium bicarbonate, NaHCO3, reacts with acetic acid?
W) Oxygen
X) Hydrogen
Y) Carbon dioxide
Z) Nitrogen
ANSWER: Y) CARBON DIOXIDE
```

<Steps>

1. Click the question box, the large text area at the top of the window. Press Command-V, or click **Paste**.
2. Press Command-Return. Or click **Speak**.
3. Listen. The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s."

</Steps>

You hear "Toss-up number one. Chemistry. Multiple choice." followed by the question and its four choices.

<Figure
  src="window-light.svg"
  dark="window-dark.svg"
  alt="The Cloudy Reader window with the sample toss-up pasted, a voice selected, rotation off, the spoken script open, and the status Playing 19.3 s."
  caption="The window during a reading, with a different time in the status bar than yours."
  frame="window"
/>

Click **Spoken script** to read the words the voice received. In this question, NaHCO3 appears as "N A H C O three". The app does not read the answer line unless you select **Read answer line**.

:::note
**Online lookups** is on by default and can send a detected chemical, protein, or gene name to PubChem or UniProt, never the question.
The answers from those services do not change what you hear, and you can deselect **Online lookups**.
See [What uses the network](/about/privacy/#what-uses-the-network).
:::

If you hear nothing, see [You hear nothing](/manual/troubleshooting/#you-hear-nothing). If **Speak** is dimmed, see [Speak is dimmed](/manual/troubleshooting/#speak-is-dimmed). If "Preparing…" does not end, see [The first reading is slow](/manual/troubleshooting/#the-first-reading-is-slow).
