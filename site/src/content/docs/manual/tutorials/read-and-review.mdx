You paste a question, play it, stop and repeat it, read the spoken script, and hear the answer line. At the end, you can run a full reading from the keyboard and check what the voice received. This tutorial takes about 10 minutes and needs Cloudy Reader open with a voice installed.

## Before you start

Open Cloudy Reader and check that the **Voice** menu shows a voice. If the menu is dimmed, no voice is installed yet: see [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed). If you have not installed the app yet, start with [Get started](/manual/getting-started/).

Check that **Rotation** shows **No rotation** and that **Read answer line** is deselected. You select it in a later step.

Set the Mac's volume before you start. The app has no volume control.

## Paste a question

The question box sits at the top of the window. Below it are the **Voice** and **Rotation** menus, the **Read answer line** and **Online lookups** checkboxes, and a row of buttons. The **Spoken script** control and the status bar are at the bottom.

Copy this question.

```text wrap
TOSS-UP
1) CHEMISTRY Multiple Choice Which gas forms when sodium bicarbonate, NaHCO3, reacts with acetic acid?
W) Oxygen
X) Hydrogen
Y) Carbon dioxide
Z) Nitrogen
ANSWER: Y) CARBON DIOXIDE
```

<Steps>

1. Select the question above and press Command-C.
2. Click **Paste** in the Cloudy Reader window.

   The question box shows the text, starting with TOSS-UP. **Paste** replaces any text that was in the box, so the box holds the question once.

</Steps>

To paste with the keyboard instead, click the question box, press Command-A to select its text, and press Command-V. Command-V alone adds the text at the cursor and leaves the old text in the box.

## Listen and control playback

The keyboard shortcuts work while Cloudy Reader is the active app.

<Steps>

1. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar shows "Preparing…" with a busy bar at the right. The first reading after the app opens takes longer, because the app loads its name-detection model once. Then the status bar shows "Playing _n_ s." and you hear "Toss-up number one. Chemistry. Multiple choice." followed by the question. Do not wait for the sound to end.

2. While the sound plays, press Command-Period (.). If the sound has already ended, press Command-R to play the reading again, and then press Command-Period (.).

   The sound ends and the status bar shows "Stopped."

3. Press Command-R.

   The status bar shows "Repeating." and the whole reading plays again.

</Steps>

The "Playing" message clears when the sound ends. Each of these shortcuts has a button: **Speak**, **Stop**, and **Repeat**. **Stop** is available only while sound plays. **Repeat** is available after a reading has played.

## Check the spoken script

<Steps>

1. If the **Spoken script** view is closed, click **Spoken script**.

   The view is open below the buttons.

2. Read the text in the view.

   It shows the words that the voice received: "Toss-up number one. Chemistry. Multiple choice. Which gas forms when sodium bicarbonate, N A H C O three, reacts with acetic acid? W. Oxygen X. Hydrogen Y. Carbon dioxide Z. Nitrogen"

</Steps>

  caption="The script spells the formula letter by letter."

Compare the script with the question you pasted:

- `TOSS-UP` and `1)` became "Toss-up number one."
- `NaHCO3` became "N A H C O three", so the voice spells the formula.
- `W)`, `X)`, `Y)`, and `Z)` became "W.", "X.", "Y.", and "Z."
- The answer line is not in the script.

## Hear the answer

<Steps>

1. Select **Read answer line**.

   The checkbox shows selected.

2. Press Command-Return.

   After the last choice, the app is silent for 5 seconds. Then you hear "Answer. Y. Carbon Dioxide." The **Spoken script** now ends with that sentence.

</Steps>

The app saves this choice. Deselect **Read answer line** to skip the answer again.

## Read two questions in a row

Copy these two questions. A paste can hold several questions.

```text wrap
TOSS-UP
2) PHYSICS Short Answer A cart moves 90 meters in 6 seconds at a constant speed. What is its speed in m/s?
ANSWER: 15

BONUS
2) PHYSICS Short Answer What is the kinetic energy, in joules, of a 2 kg cart that moves at 3 m/s?
ANSWER: 9
```

<Steps>

1. Deselect **Read answer line**.
2. Select the two questions above and press Command-C.
3. Switch to Cloudy Reader with Command-Tab, or click its window.
4. Press Command-Shift-V.

   The question box now holds both questions. The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s."

5. Look at the **Spoken script**.

   It shows the toss-up first, beginning "Toss-up number two. Physics. Short answer." The unit `m/s` became "meters per second". The bonus follows, beginning "Bonus number two. Physics. Short answer." The app pauses 1.5 seconds between the two questions.

</Steps>

:::tip
Command-Shift-V is **Speak Clipboard**. It replaces the question with the clipboard text and speaks it in one step.
:::

## What you learned

- Command-Return speaks the question, Command-Period (.) stops the sound, and Command-R plays the last reading again.
- The **Spoken script** shows the words the voice receives, such as a formula spelled out letter by letter.
- **Read answer line** adds the answer after a pause of 5 seconds.
- One paste can hold several questions. The app reads them in order.
- Command-Shift-V pastes the clipboard and speaks it in one step.
