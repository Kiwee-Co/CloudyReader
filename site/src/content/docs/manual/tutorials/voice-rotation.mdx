You choose a voice, then switch on rotation so the app picks a different voice for each reading. At the end, you can name the voice of every reading from the status bar. This tutorial takes about 10 minutes and needs the first-launch download to be finished.

## Before you start

Let the first-launch download finish before you start. During the download, the **Voice** menu lists only the first voice to arrive, and the other voices appear as the download finishes. See [Manage voices and storage](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/) to check which voices are installed.

Set the Mac's volume before you start. The app has no volume control.

## Choose a voice

The **Voice** menu groups voices under a heading for each [voice engine](/manual/reference/glossary/#voice-engine). The headings are labels, not choices. Each row shows the voice's name, its language, and a quality label.

Copy this question. You use it in every step of this tutorial.

```text wrap
TOSS-UP
1) CHEMISTRY Multiple Choice Which gas forms when sodium bicarbonate, NaHCO3, reacts with acetic acid?
W) Oxygen
X) Hydrogen
Y) Carbon dioxide
Z) Nitrogen
ANSWER: Y) CARBON DIOXIDE
```

<Steps>

1. Select the question above and press Command-C.
2. Click **Paste** in the Cloudy Reader window.

   The question box shows the question, starting with TOSS-UP.

3. Open the **Voice** menu and choose a voice other than the one it shows.

   The **Voice** menu shows the voice you chose.

4. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s." You hear the question in the voice you chose.

</Steps>

The app saves your choice and selects the same voice at the next start.

## Turn on Round robin

**Round robin** reads each question with the next voice in the **Voice** menu.

<Figure
  src="rotation-light.svg"
  dark="rotation-dark.svg"
  alt="The Rotation menu open with six modes: No rotation, Round robin, Random shuffle, US only (random shuffle), Men only, and Women only."
  caption="The Rotation menu offers six modes; No rotation is the default."
  frame="window"
/>

<Steps>

1. Open the **Rotation** menu and choose **Round robin**.

   The **Rotation** menu shows Round robin.

2. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar shows "Preparing with _voice_…", then "Playing _n_ s with _voice_." The _voice_ is a name and a gender. It is the first voice in the **Voice** menu when you have not used rotation since the app started. Otherwise it is the voice after the last one that rotation used.

3. When the sound starts, press Command-Return again.

   The status bar names the next voice in the **Voice** menu.

4. When the sound starts, press Command-Return a third time.

   The status bar names the voice after that.

</Steps>

The **Voice** menu still shows the voice you chose, because rotation never changes it.

## Try a random deck

**Random shuffle** deals the voices like a shuffled deck. No voice repeats until every voice has played.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Rotation** menu and choose **Random shuffle**.

   The **Rotation** menu shows Random shuffle.

2. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar names a voice.

3. When the sound starts, press Command-Return again.

   The status bar names a different voice.

4. When the sound starts, press Command-Return a third time.

   The status bar names a third voice that differs from the first two.

</Steps>

## Practice with one group of voices

Three modes deal from a smaller group of voices: **Men only**, **Women only**, and **US only (random shuffle)**.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Rotation** menu and choose **Men only**.

   The **Rotation** menu shows Men only.

2. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar names a voice followed by "(male)", for example "Daniel (male)".

3. When the sound starts, press Command-Return again.

   The status bar names another voice followed by "(male)".

</Steps>

Choose **Women only** and repeat the steps to see "(female)".

The status bar does not show an accent. To check **US only (random shuffle)**, look up the voice name in the **Voice** menu: every row it deals from says en-US.

:::note
The gender labels come from the voice data, and the project has not verified every label by ear. [Voices and voice engines](/manual/concepts/voice-engines/) explains the labels.
:::

If no installed voice fits the mode, the reading uses the voice in the **Voice** menu. The status bar shows a warning that stays after the sound ends.

## Repeat without changing the voice

<Steps>

1. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar names a voice. Note it.

2. When the sound ends, press Command-R.

   The status bar shows "Repeating." and you hear the same voice again.

3. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar names a different voice.

</Steps>

**Repeat** replays the stored audio, so it does not move rotation to the next voice.

## Turn rotation off

<Steps>

1. Open the **Rotation** menu and choose **No rotation**.

   The **Rotation** menu shows No rotation.

2. Press Command-Return.

   The status bar shows "Playing _n_ s." with no voice name. You hear the voice that the **Voice** menu shows.

</Steps>

The app saves the rotation mode and restores it at the next start. It does not save the deck or the last voice.

## What you learned

- The **Voice** menu groups voices under a heading for each voice engine, and each row shows a quality label.
- **Round robin** takes the next voice in menu order. **Random shuffle** deals every voice once before any voice repeats.
- **Men only**, **Women only**, and **US only (random shuffle)** deal from a smaller group.
- The status bar names the voice of each rotation reading while the sound plays.
- **Repeat** replays the last audio and does not move rotation on.
