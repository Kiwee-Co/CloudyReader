Cloudy Reader needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS REL-MACOS-FLOOR or later.

1. [Download](/download/) the zip (REL-ZIP-SIZE). It appears in your **Downloads** folder.
2. Double-click the zip, then drag **Cloudy Reader** to **Applications**. Cloudy Reader is in the Applications folder.
3. Open Cloudy Reader and click **Done**, not the Trash button, when macOS [blocks the first open](/manual/tasks/first-open/). In **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway**, then open the app again and click **Open** to see the window.
4. Keep the window open and the Mac online while the voices download, about REL-DOWNLOAD-SIZE. In about 1 minute, the **Voice** menu shows a voice, and **Speak** is no longer dimmed. If no voice appears, see [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed).
5. Paste any question, or the sample on [Get started](/manual/getting-started/#hear-a-sample-question), and press Command-Return. The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s.", and you hear the question.
