Cloudy Reader reads National Science Bowl questions aloud on your Mac.

Cloudy Reader needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS REL-MACOS-FLOOR or later. Check yours in **Apple menu > About This Mac**.

1. [Download the zip](/download/) (REL-ZIP-SIZE).
2. Double-click the zip, unless your browser already unzipped it. Drag **Cloudy Reader** to **Applications** before you open it.
3. Approve the app. The app is signed ad hoc and is not notarized, so macOS asks you to approve the [first launch](/manual/tasks/first-open/) once.
   - Open Cloudy Reader once, then close the warning dialog. Do not move the app to the Trash.
   - Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, and scroll to **Security**.
   - Click **Open Anyway**. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   - Open the app again and click **Open**. The window opens.
   - If macOS asks for an administrator's name and password, ask the person who manages the Mac.
4. Keep the window open and the Mac online while the voices download, about REL-DOWNLOAD-SIZE. A voice is what reads the question aloud. The first voice usually arrives in under a minute. The **Voice** menu then shows it, and **Speak** is no longer dimmed. If no voice arrives, see [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed).
5. Copy the [sample toss-up](/manual/getting-started/#hear-a-sample-question). Click the question box, press Command-V, then press Command-Return. The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s.", and you hear the question.

[Get started](/manual/getting-started/) explains each step.
