Cloudy Reader needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS REL-MACOS-FLOOR or later.

1. [Download the zip](/download/) (REL-ZIP-SIZE). It appears in your **Downloads** folder.
2. Double-click the zip, then drag **Cloudy Reader** to **Applications** before you open it. The app is in the **Applications** folder.
3. Open Cloudy Reader. When macOS [blocks the first launch](/manual/tasks/first-open/), click **Done**, not the button that moves the app to the Trash. In **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway**, open the app again, and click **Open**. The window opens.
4. Keep the window open and the Mac online while the voices download, about REL-DOWNLOAD-SIZE. The first voice usually arrives in under a minute. The **Voice** menu then shows it, and **Speak** is no longer dimmed. If no voice arrives, see [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed).
5. Copy any question, or the sample on [Get started](/manual/getting-started/#hear-a-sample-question). Click the question box, press Command-V, then press Command-Return. The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s.", and you hear the question.

[Get started](/manual/getting-started/) explains each step.
