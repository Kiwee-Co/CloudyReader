This page lists every control in the Cloudy Reader window, when each button is available, and what the app remembers.

## Window layout

The window uses the native macOS look. It follows the light or dark appearance and the Increase contrast setting of the system. The figure numbers its parts from top to bottom.

<Figure
  src="window-tour-light.svg"
  dark="window-tour-dark.svg"
  alt="The Cloudy Reader window with a pasted question. Numbered markers point to its seven parts, listed below the figure."
  caption="The seven parts of the main window."
  frame="window"
  callouts="window-tour"
/>

1. Question box: holds the text to read.
2. **Spoken script**: shows the words that the voice speaks, and fills as you add text.
3. **Voice**: a pop-up menu that chooses the voice.
4. **Rotation**: a pop-up menu that chooses how **Speak** picks the next voice.
5. **Options**: the checkboxes **Read answer line** and **Online lookups**.
6. **Repeat**, **Stop**, and **Speak**: play a reading again, stop the sound, and start a reading.
7. Status bar: shows messages, the download progress, and a busy bar that hides during a download or while the macOS setting Reduce Motion is on.

The question box takes plain text. While it is empty, it shows the placeholder "Paste a National Science Bowl question, then press ⌘↵ to hear it." Tab moves focus out of the box instead of typing a tab. To replace the text, click the box, press Command-A, and press Command-V.

The window has no speed control and no volume control. Every reading uses the voice's normal speed, and the sound plays at the macOS output volume.

## Buttons and when they are available

**Repeat**, **Stop**, and **Speak** sit at the right of the window, with the same width. **Speak** is the default button, so it has the accent color while the window is active. The Reading menu commands of the same names are available under the same conditions.

| Button     | What it does                                                 | Available when                                         | Tooltip                          |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| **Repeat** | Plays the last reading again. It does not prepare a reading. | A reading has played and no reading is in preparation. | Play the last reading again (⌘R) |
| **Stop**   | Stops the sound.                                             | Sound is playing.                                      | Stop playback (⌘.)               |
| **Speak**  | Speaks the question box.                                     | A voice is selected and no reading is in preparation.  | Speak the question (⌘↵)          |

Before a voice is installed, and while the app lists the voices, the three buttons are dimmed. If the question box has no text, **Speak** shows "Paste a question first."

A reading that becomes ready while a sound still plays replaces that sound. For the keys of each button, see [Keyboard shortcuts](/manual/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Voice and Rotation menus

**Voice** and **Rotation** are pop-up menus in the window, not menus in the menu bar.

| Control      | What it holds                                                                                                                                 | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Voice**    | The voices, under one heading for each voice engine. The macOS voices come last.                                                              | The headings are labels, not choices. A voice that cannot run is dimmed and cannot be chosen, and its row ends with the reason: see [Dimmed voices and their reasons](/manual/reference/voices/#dimmed-voices-and-their-reasons). For the rows, the order, and the default voice, see [Voices and rotation modes](/manual/reference/voices/#menu-order-and-the-default-voice). |
| **Speaker**  | A number box on its own row under **Voice**.                                                                                                  | It shows only for a voice with more than one speaker, and none of the supported voices has more than one. It counts from 0 and returns to 0 when you change the voice.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Rotation** | The six rotation modes: **No rotation**, **Round robin**, **Random shuffle**, **US only (random shuffle)**, **Men only**, and **Women only**. | The tooltip reads "How Speak picks the next voice." For the rules of each mode, see [Rotation modes](/manual/reference/voices/#rotation-modes).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

**Voice** and **Rotation** have the same width. The closed **Voice** menu shows the label of the chosen voice: the name, the accent and gender where known, the voice engine, and the precision. [Voice labels](/manual/reference/voices/#voice-labels) explains each field.

The **Voice** menu is dimmed in three states. It then shows one of these texts.

| Voice menu text    | When it shows                                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Loading voices…    | The app lists the voices at the start.                        |
| No voice installed | No voice is installed.                                        |
| Voices unavailable | The voice list failed to load. The status bar shows an error. |

## Options

Two checkboxes stand one below the other. The label **Options** sits to the left of the first. The app saves both choices.

| Option               | Default  | What it does                                                                                                          | Tooltip                                                    |
| -------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Read answer line** | Cleared  | Reads the answer line aloud after the question. The reading pauses for 5 seconds, then says "Answer." and the answer. | Also read the ANSWER line aloud.                           |
| **Online lookups**   | Selected | Allows the app to look up detected chemical, protein, and gene names online. The answers do not change what you hear. | Look up chemical and protein names on PubChem and UniProt. |

Clearing **Online lookups** is not [offline mode](/manual/reference/glossary/#offline-mode). Offline mode also stops the voice download: see [Work offline](/manual/tasks/work-offline/). For what a lookup sends, see [Privacy](/about/privacy/#what-uses-the-network). For what the app drops from an answer line, see [Question format and reading order](/manual/reference/question-format/).

## Spoken script

The **Spoken script** view sits under the question box. It is a read-only view of the words that the voice receives, and it fills as you add text. The view shows the [spoken script](/manual/reference/glossary/#spoken-script) for the text in the box about 150 milliseconds after the last change to the box or to **Read answer line**. The preview is offline: it uses no name detection and no online lookups, so the wording of a chemical name can change when **Speak** runs.

| Part                     | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Spoken script** button | Shows or hides the view. Click the arrow or the title to toggle it; Space does the same while the button has focus. The view is open at every start, because the app does not save the open or closed state.                                            |
| **Spoken script** view   | Read-only. Shows the spoken script of the question box, with the parts joined by single spaces. After **Speak** it shows the script of that reading. A word with a custom pronunciation shows as printed, and the voice receives its sounds separately. |
| Placeholder              | "The words that are spoken appear here." shows while the question box is empty or holds no question. It also shows for a text of more than 20,000 characters until you press **Speak**.                                                                 |

For how the app builds the script, see [How a question becomes speech](/manual/concepts/how-reading-works/).

## Status bar

The status bar sits at the bottom of the window. The figure numbers its parts during a voice download.

<Figure
  src="status-busy-download-light.svg"
  dark="status-busy-download-dark.svg"
  alt="The status bar while a reading is prepared during a voice download: Preparing… on the left, then the download label with its percentage and its progress bar."
  caption="The three parts of the status bar."
  callouts="status-busy-download"
/>

1. Message: one message at a time.
2. Download label: the download of one voice engine, as a percentage and sizes.
3. Download progress bar: the same percentage, as a bar.

The table gives the place and the content of each part. The figure has no busy bar, because the busy bar hides during a download.

| Part                  | Where                   | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Message               | Left                    | One message at a time. A warning or an error has an icon before the text, and an information message has none. The app cuts long text with an ellipsis, and the tooltip holds the full text.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Download label        | Right                   | "Downloading _engine_… _N_% (_x_ of _y_ MB)" while a voice engine's files download. _N_ and the sizes count only those files. A group over 1 GB reads in GB, as in "829 MB of 2.0 GB". In a narrow window the label drops its sizes, then its verb, and reads "Kokoro 42%". Its tooltip shows the file in progress and the percentage of all downloads. Failed files count as done in the label and in the tooltip, so check the **Voice** menu for the voices that arrived. |
| Download progress bar | Right, beside the label | A bar from 0 to 100 percent for the engine that downloads. It shows the same percentage as the label.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Busy bar              | Far right               | A bar without a percentage. It shows while the app prepares a reading and while it lists the voices. It hides during a download, and while the macOS setting Reduce Motion is on.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

The label and the bar stay until all downloads end. The **Voice** menu adds the voices of an engine when that engine's download ends.

A warning or an error shows an icon before its text, so color never carries the meaning alone.

<Figure
  src="status-warning-light.svg"
  dark="status-warning-dark.svg"
  alt="The status bar with a warning icon before the warning text."
  caption="A warning shows an icon before the text."
/>

A message stays until a later message replaces it. These messages clear on their own.

| Message                       | It clears when               |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Loading voices…               | The voice list loads.        |
| No voice is installed.        | A voice appears in the list. |
| Playing _n_ s. and Repeating. | The sound ends.              |

A download result shows only when the message area is empty. For every message, what it means, and what to do, see [Status messages](/manual/reference/status-messages/).

## Menus

The menu bar shows the **Cloudy Reader** menu, then File, Edit, Reading, and Window. macOS moves **About Cloudy Reader** and **Quit Cloudy Reader** into the **Cloudy Reader** menu, so the menu bar has no Help menu.

The app has no View menu and no Voice menu, and no command opens this manual. The keys of each command are in [Keyboard shortcuts](/manual/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/). The figure shows the Reading menu.

<Figure
  src="menu-reading-light.svg"
  dark="menu-reading-dark.svg"
  alt="The menu bar with the Reading menu open: Speak, then Repeat and Stop, each with its keyboard shortcut."
  caption="The Reading menu lists the three reading commands and their keys."
/>

The table lists every command that the app defines, the menu where it appears, and when it is available.

| Command                 | Menu          | What it does                                                                                   | Available when                                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **About Cloudy Reader** | Cloudy Reader | Opens the About panel.                                                                         | Always.                                             |
| **Quit Cloudy Reader**  | Cloudy Reader | Does the same as **Close Window**.                                                             | Always.                                             |
| **Close Window**        | File          | Closes the window and ends the app. Playback stops, and a running voice download is cancelled. | Always.                                             |
| **Undo**                | Edit          | Undoes the last edit.                                                                          | The focused field has an edit to undo.              |
| **Redo**                | Edit          | Redoes the last undone edit.                                                                   | The focused field has an undone edit.               |
| **Cut**                 | Edit          | Moves the selected text to the clipboard.                                                      | Text is selected in an editable field.              |
| **Copy**                | Edit          | Copies the selected text to the clipboard.                                                     | Text is selected.                                   |
| **Paste**               | Edit          | Inserts the clipboard text at the cursor.                                                      | The field is editable and the clipboard holds text. |
| **Select All**          | Edit          | Selects all the text in the field.                                                             | The field is not empty.                             |
| **Speak**               | Reading       | Same as the **Speak** button.                                                                  | As the button.                                      |
| **Repeat**              | Reading       | Same as the **Repeat** button.                                                                 | As the button.                                      |
| **Stop**                | Reading       | Same as the **Stop** button.                                                                   | As the button.                                      |
| **Minimize**            | Window        | Minimizes the window.                                                                          | Always.                                             |
| **Zoom**                | Window        | Switches the window between the maximized and the normal size.                                 | Always.                                             |

While a reading is in preparation, **Close Window** and **Quit Cloudy Reader** hide the window at once. The app quits when the preparation ends, and that reading does not play.

The Edit commands act on the question box, the **Spoken script** view, or the **Speaker** box, whichever has focus. With focus on any other control, all six are dimmed. The **Spoken script** view is read-only, so only **Copy** and **Select All** can act there. To replace the whole question, select all with Command-A, then paste with Command-V.

macOS adds items that the app does not define, so the table omits them. The Edit menu gains **AutoFill**, **Start Dictation**, and **Emoji & Symbols**. The **Cloudy Reader** menu gains **Services**, **Hide Cloudy Reader** (Command-H), **Hide Others** (Option-Command-H), and **Show All**.

## What the app remembers

The app has no settings window. It saves five choices and restores them at the next start.

| Saved choice             | Saved when              | At the next start                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Voice                    | You choose a voice.     | The saved voice, if it is installed. Otherwise the first installed voice in the menu. If the saved voice is dimmed with a reason, the status bar says why: see [Status messages](/manual/reference/status-messages/). |
| Rotation mode            | You choose a mode.      | The saved mode.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Read answer line**     | You select or clear it. | The saved state.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Online lookups**       | You select or clear it. | The saved state.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Window size and position | You close the window.   | The saved size and position.                                                                                                                                                                                          |

The app does not save the **Speaker** number, the rotation deck and the last voice used, or whether the **Spoken script** view is open. The key names are in [Files, settings, and limits](/manual/reference/files-and-settings/#preferences). To clear the saved choices, see [Reset the app](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/#reset-the-app).

## About Cloudy Reader

The About panel shows the app icon, the name, the version, and a one-line description. It has no buttons.

| Item        | Detail                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Opens with  | The **About Cloudy Reader** command in the **Cloudy Reader** menu.                                                                            |
| Text        | "Cloudy Reader", then "Version 0.0.3", then "Reads National Science Bowl questions aloud."                                                    |
| Closes with | The close button, Escape, or Command-W.                                                                                                       |
| Behavior    | The panel does not block the main window. One About panel opens at a time, and choosing the command again brings the open panel to the front. |
