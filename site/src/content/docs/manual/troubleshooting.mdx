Find the symptom, read the cause, and apply the fix. Quoted text is what Cloudy Reader shows, character for character. An italic letter or word, such as _n_, stands for a value that changes.

The status bar at the bottom of the window shows most messages. A warning or an error has an icon before its text, and an error shows "Error:" and the first line of its message. The app cuts a long message short. Point to the status bar to read all of it in a tooltip.

The entries follow the order in which problems appear: installing and opening, voices, reading, and sound. The last section covers what to do when no entry helps.

## Installing and opening

### Your Mac is not supported

The app does not open on your Mac.

**Cause.** Cloudy Reader needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26.2 or later. [Check your Mac](/manual/getting-started/#check-your-mac) lists the requirements. There is no Intel build.

**Fix.** Choose **Apple menu > About This Mac** and read the chip and the macOS version.

- Intel-based Mac: the release has no Intel build. Use a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
- macOS older than 26.2: choose **System Settings > General > Software Update** and install macOS 26.2 or later. If the Mac does not offer it, the Mac cannot run the app.

### The checksum does not match

The check in Terminal does not print the "OK" line.

**Cause.** The check fails for one of three reasons. The download is damaged, the command ran outside the folder that holds the download, or the hash in the command has a typing error.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Compare the size of your download with the size that [Download Cloudy Reader](/download/) lists. A different size means that your file is not the published file.
2. Run the check again from the folder that holds the download. Copy the command from that page, because a typing error in the hash fails the check.
3. If the check still fails, delete your download and get it again from that page. Then run the check again.
4. If the check fails on a second download, do not open the app. Follow [Report a security issue](/manual/get-help/#report-a-security-issue).

</Steps>

### macOS cannot verify the app

macOS blocks the first launch and says that it cannot verify the app. The wording differs between macOS versions.

**Cause.** Cloudy Reader is signed ad hoc and is not notarized. macOS marks a downloaded copy as quarantined, and Gatekeeper, the macOS check for downloaded apps, then asks you to approve the first launch. The message is expected for this app.

**Fix.** Approve the app once in **System Settings > Privacy & Security**. [Approve the app](/manual/tasks/first-open/#approve-the-app) lists the steps.

:::caution
Approve only a copy that comes from the release page and passes the checksum check. [Download Cloudy Reader](/download/) explains the check.
:::

### Open Anyway is missing

**Open Anyway** does not appear in **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.

**Cause.** macOS shows the button only after it blocks an attempt to open the app.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Open Cloudy Reader again from the **Applications** folder, then close the warning dialog.
2. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** at once, and scroll to **Security**.
3. Click **Open Anyway**.
4. If the button is still missing on a school or work Mac, ask the administrator to approve the app. Do not work around the policy.

</Steps>

[If Open Anyway is missing](/manual/tasks/first-open/#if-open-anyway-is-missing) has the details.

### macOS says the app is damaged

macOS says that the app is damaged.

**Cause.** A file inside the app may have changed, for example in a damaged download.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Run the checksum check on your download again. [Download Cloudy Reader](/download/) has the command.
2. Test the app with [Check that the app is intact](/manual/tasks/first-open/#check-that-the-app-is-intact). That section also says what to do when the app fails the test.
3. If the app passes, approve it with [Approve the app](/manual/tasks/first-open/#approve-the-app).

</Steps>

Advanced users can [remove the quarantine flag](/manual/tasks/first-open/#remove-the-quarantine-flag). Do that only for an app that you trust, and never against the policy of a managed Mac.

## Voices

The app downloads its voices at the first launch. [Manage voices and storage](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/) shows how to check and retry the download.

### No voice is installed

The status bar shows "No voice is installed." The **Voice** menu is dimmed and reads "No voice installed", and **Speak** is dimmed.

**Cause.** The Mac has no installed voice. Three cases apply:

- The download is still running. This is normal at the first launch. The first voice arrives before the rest of the download ends.
- The download failed, for example on a blocked network or a full disk. A failed download shows no message, because the app shows a download result only when the status bar has no other message. The app tries the missing files again at the next start.
- [Offline mode](/manual/reference/glossary/#offline-mode) skipped the download. The window never says so.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Look at the right end of the status bar. If "Downloading voices… _N_%" shows, keep the window open until a voice appears in the **Voice** menu. Closing the window stops the download.
2. If the percentage and the progress bar disappear and the menu stays empty, the download failed. If no percentage ever shows, offline mode may have skipped it. In both cases, check the internet connection and the free disk space.
3. Quit the app with Command-Q and open it from the **Applications** folder. The download starts at each launch, and this start is not in offline mode.
4. If the download fails again, see [The voice download does not progress](#the-voice-download-does-not-progress).

</Steps>

[Start in offline mode](/manual/tasks/work-offline/#start-in-offline-mode) explains how offline mode starts and ends.

### The voice download does not progress

The status bar shows "Downloading voices… 0%" for a while, or the percentage climbs and no voice appears in the **Voice** menu.

**Cause.** The Mac does not receive the voice files. The app tries each file 3 times, with waits of 2 and then 4 seconds between the tries. The percentage counts a file as finished when its tries end, even when every try failed.

With no connection, the status bar behaves like this:

- The percentage stays at 0 while the first small files fail, and then it climbs in steps.
- Each failed file takes about 6 seconds, so a run in which every file fails takes minutes.
- When the run ends, the percentage and the progress bar disappear, and no voice has arrived.
- A connection that hangs takes longer, because each try can wait up to 10 seconds to connect.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Open the **Voice** menu. The download works when the first voice appears in the menu. A rising percentage is not proof, because failed files count as finished.
2. If the percentage and the progress bar have disappeared and the menu is empty, the download failed. Check the Wi-Fi and any VPN.
3. On a school or work network, ask the administrator to allow the download. [What uses the network](/about/privacy/#what-uses-the-network) lists the hosts.
4. When the connection works, quit the app with Command-Q and open it again. On a slow connection, keep the window open until the first voice appears.

</Steps>

### Some voices could not be downloaded

The status bar shows the warning "Some voices could not be downloaded. They are tried again at the next start."

**Cause.** A file failed all 3 tries, or it failed in a way that a retry cannot fix. A full disk and a file that does not match its expected SHA-256 value are two examples. The app shows this warning only when the status bar has no other message, so a message from a reading can hide it. After a failed first download, "No voice is installed." fills the status bar and this warning does not appear: see [No voice is installed](#no-voice-is-installed).

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Point to the warning. The tooltip lists the names of the files that failed.
2. Check the internet connection and the free disk space. [Free disk space](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/#free-disk-space) explains how to free space.
3. Quit the app with Command-Q and open it again. The app downloads only the missing files.
4. If the same file fails again, [report the problem](/manual/get-help/#report-a-problem) and name the file.

</Steps>

### Few voices are in the Voice menu at first

During the first download, the **Voice** menu lists only the first voice to arrive.

**Cause.** The app updates the **Voice** menu when the first voice arrives and again when the whole download ends. The other voices stay out of the menu until then, even after their files arrive.

**Fix.** Wait until the download finishes: the percentage and the progress bar disappear. The menu then lists every voice. [Voices and rotation modes](/manual/reference/voices/) lists them all, and [Manage voices and storage](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/) shows how to check the download.

### Voices from one engine are missing

The **Voice** menu has no heading for one voice engine, or the rows of one engine are dimmed and their labels contain "not installed".

**Cause.** The files of that engine are not all on the Mac. The app lists an engine's voices only when the engine's files are present and valid. Some engines list all their voices and dim the voices whose own file is missing. Three cases apply:

- The download has not finished. The menu may not list an engine's voices until the whole download ends.
- The download stopped early. Closing the window, losing the connection, or starting in offline mode stops it.
- A file is damaged. The download does not replace a file that is already there, so it never fetches that file again.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Wait until the percentage and the progress bar disappear. If no download runs, check the internet connection. Then quit the app with Command-Q and open it again.
2. If Kokoro voices are still missing, quit the app. In Finder, choose **Go > Go to Folder** and paste `~/Library/Application Support/cloudyreader/models`. This is the `models` folder in the [data folder](/manual/reference/files-and-settings/#data-folder). Move the `kokoro` folder to the Trash.
3. Open the app with the Mac online. The download fetches the missing files again.

</Steps>

For a Piper voice, see [A voice error appears](#a-voice-error-appears).

### The Voice menu cannot list the voices

The **Voice** menu reads "Voices unavailable", and the status bar shows an error that starts with "Error:".

**Cause.** The app could not build the list of voices. The status bar shows the reason after "Error:".

**Fix.** Point to the status bar to read the whole error. Quit the app with Command-Q and open it again. If the menu still reads "Voices unavailable", [report the problem](/manual/get-help/#report-a-problem) and include the error text.

### The voice changes by itself

The status bar shows "Preparing with _name_ (_gender_)…", or the **Voice** menu moves to another voice.

**Cause.** One of three cases applies:

- Rotation is on. With any mode except **No rotation**, **Speak** takes the voice from the rotation pool for each reading and not from the **Voice** menu.
- You never chose a voice. Until you do, the app selects the first installed voice in the **Voice** menu. When the download finishes, the selection moves to the first voice in the menu.
- A rotation mode has no voice that fits. The status bar shows "No installed voice matches “Men only”; using the selected voice." The app then uses the voice in the **Voice** menu.

**Fix.**

- Rotation: choose **No rotation** in the **Rotation** menu. [Rotation modes](/manual/reference/voices/#rotation-modes) explains each mode.
- No chosen voice: choose the voice that you want in the **Voice** menu. The app saves the choice for the next start.
- No voice fits: wait until the download ends, or choose another rotation mode.

## Reading

### Speak is dimmed

**Speak** and **Speak Clipboard** are dimmed, and Command-Return and Command-Shift-V do nothing.

**Cause.** Both buttons need a selected voice and no reading in preparation. A voice is missing, the voice list is still loading, or a reading is still in preparation.

**Fix.** Match the status bar to one case:

- "Loading voices…" with a moving bar at the right: wait until the bar stops.
- "No voice is installed.": see [No voice is installed](#no-voice-is-installed).
- "Preparing…" with a moving bar: wait for "Playing _n_ s." The first reading takes longer, as [The first reading is slow](#the-first-reading-is-slow) explains.
- The **Voice** menu reads "Voices unavailable": see [The Voice menu cannot list the voices](#the-voice-menu-cannot-list-the-voices).

**Repeat** stays dimmed until a reading has played, and **Stop** stays dimmed unless sound is playing. This is normal.

### The app finds nothing to read

The status bar shows "Paste a question first.", "Clipboard is empty.", or "Error: nothing to read".

**Cause.** Three cases apply:

- "Paste a question first." means that the question box is blank when you click **Speak**.
- "Clipboard is empty." means that the clipboard holds no text when you click **Paste** or **Speak Clipboard**.
- "Error: nothing to read" means that the pasted text holds no question. The app drops lines such as round titles, rule lines, and page footers, and a question needs a [stem](/manual/reference/glossary/#stem) or choices.

**Fix.** Copy the question text again and paste it into the question box. [Paste questions from a PDF or web page](/manual/tasks/paste-questions/) shows how to check what the app understood. [Question format and reading order](/manual/reference/question-format/) lists the text that the app accepts.

### The choices are read as part of the question

The voice reads the choices W, X, Y, and Z as part of the question, without the pauses between them.

**Cause.** A multiple-choice question needs all four choice letters. A question with three choices, such as `W) speed X) mass Y) velocity`, is not split into choices, and the letters are read as text.

**Fix.** Compare the pasted text with the source and add the missing choice. Then click **Speak**. [Question format and reading order](/manual/reference/question-format/) lists the limits.

### A voice error appears

The status bar shows an error that names a voice or a voice engine. The message starts with one of these texts:

- "Error: voice '_key_' is not installed"
- "Error: unknown voice '_key_'"
- "Error: TTS engine 'kokoro' is unavailable (voice kokoro:_id_)"
- "Error: Piper voice model not found: _path_"
- "Error: invalid Piper voice config _path_"

**Cause.** The voice that you chose cannot be used. Its files may be missing or damaged, its voice engine may not start, or the voice list may be out of date. The message names the voice by its id, such as `kokoro:af_heart`, or names the voice's file.

**Fix.**

- Choose another voice in the **Voice** menu and click **Speak**. If the voice list is out of date, quit the app with Command-Q and open it again. [Voices and rotation modes](/manual/reference/voices/) lists the menu name of each voice with its id or its file name.
- For an error about a voice engine, see [Voices from one engine are missing](#voices-from-one-engine-are-missing).
- For a Piper voice that the app downloads, quit the app with Command-Q. If the message says "invalid Piper voice config", move the file that it names to the Trash. Open the app again with the Mac online, and the download fetches the missing file.
- For a Piper voice that you added, copy its files again: [Add a Piper voice](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/#add-a-piper-voice).

### The first reading is slow

The status bar shows "Preparing…" for several seconds before the first reading plays.

**Cause.** The app starts loading its name-detection model when it opens, and the first reading waits for it. On a Mac with an M2 Ultra chip, the wait was about 6 seconds, and a slower Mac waits longer. The first reading with some voices also waits while their voice engine loads; [Voices and voice engines](/manual/concepts/voice-engines/) says which voices.

**Fix.** Wait for "Playing _n_ s." The delay happens once each time the app starts. To get it out of the way, read one short question after you open the app. To hear the last reading again at once, click **Repeat**. Reading the same question again with the same voice also starts at once, because the app keeps the audio in its audio cache.

### A reading waits when lookups are on

A reading starts up to 2.5 seconds late, and the status bar shows only "Preparing…".

**Cause.** **Online lookups** is selected by default, so the app asks PubChem or UniProt about the chemical, protein, and gene names that it detects. A reading waits up to 2.5 seconds for the answers. The answers do not change the sound, and the window shows no message about lookups.

**Fix.** Deselect **Online lookups**. The app saves the choice, and the reading sounds the same. [Work offline](/manual/tasks/work-offline/) turns off all network use. [What uses the network](/about/privacy/#what-uses-the-network) lists what the app sends.

### The app uses a lot of memory

Cloudy Reader holds a lot of memory.

**Cause.** The app keeps its models in memory while it runs.

**Fix.** Quit other apps that you do not need, and quit Cloudy Reader when you finish. [Voices and voice engines](/manual/concepts/voice-engines/) covers the memory that the voice engines use.

### A word sounds wrong

A word sounds wrong, with or without a warning in the status bar.

**Cause.** The app sets the sound of the hard science words in its built-in lexicon, and of the systematic chemical names that it can parse. For any other word, the voice follows its own rules and can mispronounce the word. A [pronunciation guide](/manual/reference/glossary/#pronunciation-guide) in the text sets the sound of one word.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. If the status bar shows a warning, point to it to read the whole text. [Status messages](/manual/reference/status-messages/) lists every warning.
2. Add a pronunciation guide in square brackets right after the word, such as `Krebs cycle [KREBZ SY-kul]`.
3. Click **Speak**. The status bar shows a warning when the app cannot use the guide. The app then speaks the word plain.

</Steps>

[Fix a mispronounced word](/manual/tutorials/fix-a-word/) teaches the three kinds of guides, and [Pronunciation guides](/manual/reference/pronunciation-guides/) lists the syntax. The app leaves some written forms as they are, such as a slash next to a letter, and a Piper voice then says "slash". [How a question becomes speech](/manual/concepts/how-reading-works/) lists these limits.

### A warning says an override surface was not found

The status bar shows "Warning: piper: override surface '_word_' not found in the segment text".

**Cause.** An override is the exact pronunciation that the built-in lexicon, a pronunciation guide, or a systematic chemical name sets for one word, called its surface. A Piper voice cannot apply an override when an apostrophe and a letter follow the word, as in `Avogadro's`. The app drops the override and speaks the word plain.

**Fix.** The reading goes on, so no action is required. To hear the pronunciation, write the word without the apostrophe and the letter, such as "Avogadro number". [Pronunciation guides](/manual/reference/pronunciation-guides/) lists the other warnings, including the ones that start with the name of a voice engine.

## Sound

### You hear nothing

The status bar shows "Playing _n_ s.", but no sound comes out.

**Cause.** The app plays sound through the macOS player `/usr/bin/afplay`. The player uses the output device and the volume that macOS has set. The app has no volume control.

**Fix.**

<Steps>

1. Play other audio on the Mac. If that is silent too, raise the volume and make sure the output is not muted.
2. Choose **System Settings > Sound** and select the output device that you want.
3. Click **Repeat** to hear the last reading again.

</Steps>

### The player cannot start

The status bar shows "Error: cannot run /usr/bin/afplay: _error_" or "Error: WAV not found: _path_".

**Cause.** The first message means that macOS did not start its player, `/usr/bin/afplay`. The text after the colon is the error that macOS returned. The second message means that the audio file of the reading is gone from the audio cache.

**Fix.**

- For "WAV not found", click **Speak**. The app prepares the reading again. Do not delete files in the data folder while the app runs.
- For "cannot run /usr/bin/afplay", quit the app with Command-Q and open it again. If the message returns, [report the problem](/manual/get-help/#report-a-problem) and include the text after the colon.

### The pauses are long with some voices

The pauses between the parts of a question sound long with some voices.

**Cause.** Some voices add their own silence around each line, so their pauses run longer than the silence that the app inserts. [Voices and voice engines](/manual/concepts/voice-engines/) explains which voices do this and why.

**Fix.** Choose another voice and compare the pauses. The app has no setting for the length of a pause.

## Still stuck

If no entry fixes the problem, collect details, reset the app, and report the problem.

<Steps>

1. Read the version and run the self-test. [Collect details](/manual/get-help/#collect-details) shows how.
2. If the self-test fails, find its line in the next entry.
3. [Reset the app](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/#reset-the-app). This removes the voices, the caches, and your choices. The app downloads its voices again at the next start.
4. [Report the problem](/manual/get-help/#report-a-problem) with your details. For a security issue, follow [Report a security issue](/manual/get-help/#report-a-security-issue).

</Steps>

### The self-test fails

The self-test prints a line that starts with `FAIL:`.

**Cause.** The self-test reads a sample question with the first Piper voice, without a window or sound. It exits with 0 when it passes, 1 when it fails, and 2 when no Piper voice is installed. [Command line](/manual/reference/command-line/) lists the whole report.

**Fix.** Find the line:

- `FAIL: no Piper voice installed` with exit code 2: the Mac has no Piper voice. Follow [No voice is installed](#no-voice-is-installed).
- `FAIL: WAV missing: …` or `FAIL: WAV unreadable: …`: the sample reading made no usable audio file. [Free disk space](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/#free-disk-space), then run the self-test again.
- `FAIL: audio too short: …` or `FAIL: audio too quiet: …`: the sample made less than 3 seconds of audio, or a peak level of 0.01 or lower. Run the self-test again. If the line returns, [reset the app](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/#reset-the-app).
- `FAIL: <Type>: <message>` after a traceback: a step raised an error. [Report the problem](/manual/get-help/#report-a-problem) and include the traceback.
