Use this page to practice alone. Cloudy Reader reads a question, you answer out loud in the pause, and then the app reads the answer line. The app does not time or score you.

## Set up a practice session

Let the voices download before your first session. See [Let the voices download](/manual/getting-started/#let-the-voices-download).

<Steps>

1. Open Cloudy Reader.
2. Select **Read answer line**. The app then reads the answer after each question.
3. Choose a voice in **Voice**, or choose a mode in **Rotation**.
4. Click the question box and press Command-V to paste your questions.
5. Press Command-Return.

</Steps>

The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s." With a rotation mode, both messages name the voice. You hear the question, a pause, and the answer. The app saves your rotation mode, **Read answer line**, and **Online lookups** for the next session. It saves a voice that you choose yourself.

The **Options** row holds the two checkboxes that matter for practice:

1. **Read answer line**: reads the answer after each question.
2. **Online lookups**: allows name lookups.

## Answer before the answer line

With **Read answer line** selected, the app pauses for 5 seconds after the question. Then it says "Answer." and the answer. Some voices add their own silence around each line, so the pause can run longer. See [Voices and voice engines](/manual/concepts/voice-engines/).

<Steps>

1. Listen to the question. The status bar shows "Playing _n_ s." while the sound plays.
2. Say your answer out loud when the question ends.
3. Listen to the answer line. It starts with "Answer."
4. Press Command-R to hear the whole reading again, or paste the next question.

</Steps>

:::note
The pause is fixed. The app does not time your answer. For answer times, use your competition's rules.
:::

The app reads the main answer and stops at the first judge note, such as `(ACCEPT:` or `(PROMPT:`. An answer in capital letters is read in title case, so the line `ANSWER: Y) CARBON DIOXIDE` is read as "Answer. Y. Carbon Dioxide." Deselect **Read answer line** to hear no answers.

## Work through a question set

To go one question at a time, use the keyboard loop:

<Steps>

1. Copy one question from your PDF. See [Paste questions from a PDF or web page](/manual/tasks/paste-questions/).
2. Press Command-Tab to switch to Cloudy Reader.
3. Click the question box, press Command-A, then press Command-V.
4. Press Command-Return.
5. Answer out loud, then hear the answer line.
6. Press Command-R to hear the question again, or go back to step 1 for the next question.

</Steps>

To read a set in one run, paste all of it and press **Speak**. The app reads the questions in order and leaves 1.5 seconds between them. You cannot skip ahead: **Stop** ends the reading, and **Repeat** starts it again from the first question. Paste a few questions at a time.

## Vary the voices

Rotation picks a voice each time a reading starts. Choose a mode in **Rotation**:

- **Round robin** goes through the voices in menu order.
- **Random shuffle** deals the voices in random order, and no voice repeats until all have played.
- **US only (random shuffle)**, **Men only**, and **Women only** deal from a smaller group of voices.

Some gender labels are guesses that nobody has checked by ear. See [Rotation modes](/manual/reference/voices/#rotation-modes) for the groups and the exact rules.

The status bar names the voice, such as Heart (female, Kokoro): "Preparing with _voice_…", then "Playing _n_ s with _voice_."

Rotation picks one voice for each reading, so a paste with several questions is read in one voice. Paste one question at a time to hear a different voice for each question. **Repeat** plays the same audio and keeps the voice.

While the first download runs, the **Voice** menu dims or omits the voices that have not arrived, and rotation picks only from the voices that are ready. Use rotation after the download label and progress bar leave the status bar.

To learn rotation step by step, see [Practice with voice rotation](/manual/tutorials/voice-rotation/).

## Practice without a network

After the first launch downloads the voices, reading needs no network.

<Steps>

1. Check that the **Voice** menu lists voices. If the menu is dimmed and empty, see [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed).
2. Deselect **Online lookups** to stop name lookups. The app saves this choice.
3. Read as usual.

</Steps>

Name lookups do not change how a question sounds. You hear the same words and pronunciations with **Online lookups** selected or deselected. To stop lookups and the voice download for one launch, see [Work offline](/manual/tasks/work-offline/).
