Use this page to see which voices the app has, fetch missing voices, add your own, and free disk space. The app keeps its voices and caches in the data folder, `~/Library/Application Support/cloudyreader`.

To open the data folder, choose **Go > Go to Folder** in Finder, paste the path, and press Return.

## Check which voices are installed

1. Open Cloudy Reader.
2. Open the **Voice** menu.

The app downloads and checks the supported voices. [Voices and rotation modes](/manual/reference/voices/) lists them and the menu order.

The menu groups the voices under the name of their [voice engine](/manual/reference/glossary/#voice-engine). The engine headings are labels, not choices. The **macOS** heading lists the English voices that macOS has. The app downloads nothing for them, and the data folder holds none of their files.

A row reads like "Lessac (US, female) · Piper · medium". It holds the name, the accent and gender where known, the engine, and the quality. [Voice labels](/manual/reference/voices/#voice-labels) explains each part. A dimmed row cannot be chosen yet, and it adds a dash and its reason after the quality.

If the menu cuts a reason short, point to the row to read the whole reason. When every voice is ready, no row is dimmed.

These are the reasons and the fix for each:

- "not downloaded yet": the download has not reached the files of this voice, or the file of a Kokoro voice is missing. Wait for the download, or retry it as [Download missing voices](#download-missing-voices) describes.
- "offline: not downloaded": offline mode skipped the download. Quit the app and open it with the network on.
- "not enough disk space: needs _x_ GB, _y_ GB free": the Mac has too little room for the missing files. [Free disk space](#free-disk-space), then quit the app and open it again.
- "the models folder is not writable": the app cannot save files in the `models` folder. Make sure your account can write to that folder.
- "damaged: _detail_": a file failed a check. Move the engine's folder from `models` to the Trash, then open the app. The download fetches its files again.
- "waiting for a Qwen3 decoder fix (_detail_)": a component of this engine has a known fault. Nothing on your Mac fixes it, so choose another voice.

The menu shows placeholder text when it has no voice to list:

- "Loading voices…": the app is listing the voices.
- "No voice installed": no voice is on the Mac, and the menu is dimmed.
- "Voices unavailable": the app could not list the voices, and the status bar shows an error.

If the voice you saved is dimmed at the start, the app selects the first installed voice in the menu. For a Chatterbox Turbo or Qwen3-TTS voice, the status bar also names the reason. See [The voice changes by itself](/manual/troubleshooting/#the-voice-changes-by-itself).

If the voices of one engine are missing from the menu, see [Troubleshooting](/manual/troubleshooting/#voices-from-one-engine-are-missing).

## Download missing voices

The app downloads missing supported voices by itself, once each time it starts. It does not fetch a file that is already present.

The app downloads one engine at a time. When an engine finishes, its voices join the **Voice** menu while the next engine downloads. The macOS voices need no download, so you can read with one of them while the first engine downloads. On a Mac that lists no voice, the first downloaded voice joins when it arrives.

To retry a download that failed or stopped:

1. Connect the Mac to the network.
2. Quit Cloudy Reader with Command-Q.
3. Open Cloudy Reader from **Applications**. Offline mode stops the download, so do not use the `--offline` option.
4. Watch the status bar for a progress bar and a label that names the engine. The label reads "Downloading _engine_… _N_% (_x_ of _y_ MB)", or "(_x_ MB of _y_ GB)" for an engine over 1 GB.
5. When the label and the bar leave the status bar, open the **Voice** menu and check the list.

The percentage and the megabytes count the files of the engine that downloads. In a narrow window, the label drops its sizes first and then the word "Downloading". Point to the label to see the file in progress and the percentage of all downloads. A file whose tries all failed still counts, so a rising percentage does not show that voices arrived.

Check the **Voice** menu, not the percentage. [The voice download does not progress](/manual/troubleshooting/#the-voice-download-does-not-progress) explains why.

The percentage and the bar leave the status bar when the download ends, whatever the result. After a complete download, the status bar shows "Voices downloaded." if it has no other message. On a Mac with no macOS voice, the status bar keeps "No voice is installed." as a warning if no voice arrived, and it shows no message about the failed files; see [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed).

Some engines have large model files. Before it downloads such an engine, the app checks that the Mac has room for the missing files, with a margin. It also checks that the `models` folder is writable. If a check fails, the app skips that engine and downloads the rest.

After the run, the status bar can show "_engine_ not downloaded: _reason_" in place of "Voices downloaded." if it has no other message. Point to the message to read every skip. After you free space or fix the folder, quit the app and open it again to try that engine again.

[Files, settings, and limits](/manual/reference/files-and-settings/#built-in-limits) lists the margin.

If some files fail on a Mac that already has a voice, the status bar can show a warning. The warning reads "Some voices could not be downloaded. They are tried again at the next start." Point to the message to read the names of the failed files, then repeat the steps above.

Within one run, a retry continues a partial file. When a file's tries end, the app deletes the partial file, and the next start fetches that file again from the beginning.

To stop a download, quit the app. It reports nothing, and the next start fetches the files that are still missing.

The download never replaces a file that is already present, even a damaged one. Move the damaged file from the data folder to the Trash and open the app again.

## Add a Piper voice

Add a Piper voice when you want a voice beyond the supported voices. A voice has two files, `<name>.onnx` and `<name>.onnx.json`.

1. Download both files from the [Piper voices repository on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices).
2. Quit Cloudy Reader with Command-Q.
3. Open the data folder, then open its `voices` folder.
4. Copy both files into `voices`.
5. Open Cloudy Reader. The voice appears under **Piper** in the **Voice** menu.

The app lists a voice only when both files are present and the `.onnx.json` file holds a JSON object. It takes the voice name from the file name, and the language and quality from the `.onnx.json` file. The menu sorts Piper voices by quality, best first, then by name.

If a voice does not appear, check that both files are in the `voices` folder, then restart the app.

When the `.onnx` file is there but its `.onnx.json` file is missing or does not hold a JSON object, the app skips the voice. If you start the app from Terminal, it prints a line that starts with "skipping voice". See [Read the lines the app prints](/manual/get-help/#read-the-lines-the-app-prints).

A voice you add has three limits:

- It never joins rotation, because rotation needs a known gender.
- The app does not read a voice file again while it runs. Restart the app after you replace a file.
- The pronunciation rules are written for English. The app lists a voice in another language, but the rules are not made for it.

## Remove a voice you added

1. Quit Cloudy Reader with Command-Q.
2. Open the data folder, then open its `voices` folder.
3. Move the voice's `.onnx` and `.onnx.json` files to the Trash.
4. Open Cloudy Reader. The voice no longer appears in the **Voice** menu.

If the removed voice was your selected voice, the app selects the first installed voice in the menu. Do not remove a supported voice this way: the app downloads it again at the next start unless offline mode is on.

## Copy voices to another Mac

Copy the voices to skip the download on the other Mac. Copy from a Mac where the voices work, because the app does not detect a damaged Piper file that has the right size. The copy covers the voices that the app downloads. The macOS voices belong to macOS, so each Mac lists its own.

The `voices` folder holds the Piper voices. The `models` folder holds one folder for each other engine that has model files.

1. On the Mac that has the voices, quit Cloudy Reader and open the data folder.
2. Copy the `voices` and `models` folders to a USB drive. Together they take about 3.05 GB.
3. On the other Mac, install Cloudy Reader but do not open it. See [Install, update, and uninstall](/manual/tasks/install/).
4. In Finder, choose **Go > Go to Folder**, type `~/Library/Application Support`, and press Return.
5. Open the `cloudyreader` folder. If it does not exist, choose **File > New Folder** and name the folder `cloudyreader`.
6. Copy the `voices` and `models` folders from the drive into the `cloudyreader` folder.
7. Open Cloudy Reader. The app downloads only the files that are missing.

An engine with large model files checks the type and size of its files whenever the app lists voices. A wrong size dims that engine's voices with the reason "damaged". The engine also checks every file with SHA-256 at its first reading. A mismatch stops that reading with an error that starts "Error: damaged: SHA-256 mismatch for".

If a check fails, quit the app and copy the engine's folder again from a Mac where the voices work. The app can reuse a failed check until it restarts. If the voices of one engine are missing or dimmed after the copy, see [Troubleshooting](/manual/troubleshooting/#voices-from-one-engine-are-missing).

## Free disk space

The audio cache in the data folder keeps one WAV file for each distinct reading. It grows without limit, and the app never cleans it. The table lists the largest folders and what happens when you delete each one.

| Folder or file        | Size                | After you delete it                             |
| --------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `cache/wav`           | Grows without limit | Each question renders again at its next reading |
| `voices` and `models` | About 3.05 GB       | The app downloads them again at the next start  |

[Files, settings, and limits](/manual/reference/files-and-settings/#data-folder) lists the size of every file. The file you downloaded to install Cloudy Reader is outside the data folder. Delete it when you no longer need it, and keep it if you plan to install the app on another Mac.

To delete the audio cache:

1. Quit Cloudy Reader with Command-Q. **Repeat** needs the stored audio, so do not delete the cache while the app runs.
2. Open the data folder, then open its `cache` folder.
3. Move the `wav` folder to the Trash, then empty the Trash.

The app creates the folder again at its next reading. Deleting voices frees space only until you open the app with a network again. An engine that the app skipped for lack of space downloads at the next start, if the Mac then has room.

## Reset the app

Reset the app to remove its voices and caches and return every choice to its default.

:::caution
Resetting deletes voices you added by hand, and the app cannot download them again. Copy their files elsewhere first.
:::

1. Quit Cloudy Reader with Command-Q.

2. In Finder, choose **Go > Go to Folder**, type `~/Library/Application Support`, and press Return.

3. Move the `cloudyreader` folder to the Trash.

4. Open Terminal from **Applications > Utilities** and run this command:

   ```bash
   defaults delete "com.cloudyreader.Cloudy Reader"
   ```

5. Open Cloudy Reader.

With a network, the app downloads the voices again and starts with these defaults:

- **Voice**: the first installed voice in the menu.
- **Rotation**: **No rotation**.
- **Read answer line**: not selected.
- **Online lookups**: selected.
- The window opens at its default size.

[Files, settings, and limits](/manual/reference/files-and-settings/#preferences) lists the saved preference keys.

To reset less, quit the app and do only one part:

- Run only the `defaults delete` command to reset the choices and keep the voices.
- Move only the `cache` folder inside the data folder to the Trash to clear both caches. The app creates it again.
