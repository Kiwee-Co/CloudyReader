Read questions with no network, at home or at a venue. The voices must be on the Mac first, because the app downloads them when it starts.

## Download the voices first

The app downloads its voices at the first launch and keeps them on the Mac, so the first launch needs a network.

1. Connect the Mac to the network.
2. Open Cloudy Reader and keep the window open until the percentage and the progress bar leave the status bar.
3. Open the **Voice** menu and check the list.

When the download succeeds, the **Voice** menu lists every supported voice, and no row says "not installed". If the menu stays empty or a row says "not installed", the download failed: quit the app and open it again with the network on. [Manage voices and storage](/manual/tasks/voices-and-storage/) shows how to follow the download and how to retry it.

:::tip
Test before the event. Turn Wi-Fi off, open Cloudy Reader, and read a question. If the question plays, the voice you chose works without a network.
:::

## Turn off name lookups

Name lookups send a detected chemical, protein, or gene name to PubChem or UniProt. The answers never change what you hear. A lookup that cannot reach the service can hold a reading for up to 2.5 seconds. Turn lookups off to skip that wait and to keep every detected name on your Mac.

The window has two options, and **Online lookups** controls name lookups.

1. **Read answer line**: not selected.
2. **Online lookups**: selected, the default.

Deselect **Online lookups**. The app stops name lookups and remembers your choice for the next launch.

## Start in offline mode

Offline mode stops name lookups and the voice download until you quit the app.

1. Quit Cloudy Reader with Command-Q. The app reads the option only when it starts.

2. Open Terminal from **Applications > Utilities**.

3. Run this command. Keep Terminal open while the app runs:

   ```bash
   "/Applications/Cloudy Reader.app/Contents/MacOS/Cloudy Reader" --offline
   ```

The Cloudy Reader window opens.

:::note
The window shows no sign of offline mode, and **Online lookups** stays as you left it. The variable `CLOUDYREADER_OFFLINE` has the same effect when you run the app by its path in Terminal. See [Command line](/manual/reference/command-line/) for the form.
:::

## Check what offline mode changes

Offline mode stops two features:

- Name lookups. PubChem and UniProt receive no request.
- The voice download. The app sends no request, creates no folder, and shows no message.

The diagram in [Privacy](/about/privacy/#figure-network-use) shows both features. Everything else works as usual:

- Reading works with every installed voice. The local OPSIN check, which parses chemical names on your Mac, keeps running.
- If no voice is installed, **Speak** stays dimmed. See [No voice is installed](/manual/troubleshooting/#no-voice-is-installed).
- Offline mode is not saved. The next normal launch is online again.

## Return to online use

1. Quit Cloudy Reader with Command-Q.
2. Open Cloudy Reader from **Applications**.
3. Select **Online lookups** if you deselected it.

The app is online again, and it downloads any voice that is missing.
