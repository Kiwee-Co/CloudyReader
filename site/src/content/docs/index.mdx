## Start here

<Card title="Onboarding guide" icon="open-book">

1. Download: get Cloudy Reader from the [Download page](/download/).
2. Install: open the disk image and drag **Cloudy Reader** to **Applications**.
3. Approve the app: allow it once in **Privacy & Security**.
4. Wait for the voices: keep the window open while they download.
5. Hear a question: paste one and press Command-Return.

[Follow the onboarding guide](/manual/onboarding/)

</Card>

## What you hear

These five inputs show how the app turns written science into spoken words.

| You paste                    | You hear                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `C6H12O6`                    | "C six H twelve O six"                                   |
| `5 m/s`                      | "5 meters per second"                                    |
| `1.6 × 10^-19 C`             | "one point six times ten to the minus nineteen coulombs" |
| `2 1/2`                      | "two and one half"                                       |
| `Krebs cycle [KREBZ SY-kul]` | "Krebs cycle", said as /ˈkɹɛbz ˈsaɪkəl/                  |

A pronunciation guide in brackets sets how the app says a word.

## Who it is for

<CardGrid>

<Card title="Students" icon="pencil">

Select **Read answer line**, hear a question, answer in the 5-second pause, then hear the answer. Choose a rotation mode to hear a different voice each time you press **Speak**.

[Practice on your own](/manual/tasks/practice-alone/)

</Card>

<Card title="Coaches" icon="list-format">

Read toss-ups and bonuses from the keyboard: copy a question, switch to Cloudy Reader, and press Command-Shift-V. The voices play on your Mac, so a venue with no network works after setup.

[Run a team practice](/manual/tasks/team-practice/)

</Card>

<Card title="Moderators" icon="comment-alt">

Hear how hard terms and formulas sound before you read them in a round. Add a pronunciation guide in brackets when a word sounds wrong.

[Pronunciation examples](/manual/reference/pronunciation-examples/)

</Card>

</CardGrid>

## What stays on your Mac

<CardGrid>

<Card title="Voices on your Mac" icon="laptop">

Voices and reading run on your Mac, and the audio never leaves it.

</Card>

<Card title="Online lookups" icon="magnifier">

**Online lookups** is on by default. It can send one detected chemical, protein, or gene name at a time to PubChem or UniProt, never the text around it. You can deselect it.

</Card>

<Card title="No telemetry" icon="padlock">

The app has no telemetry, analytics, crash reports, or update checks.

</Card>

</CardGrid>

Read the full statement in [Privacy](/about/privacy/).

## Requirements

Cloudy Reader needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26.2 or later. See [Check your Mac](/manual/getting-started/#check-your-mac) for disk space and network needs.
