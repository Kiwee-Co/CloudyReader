Cloudy Reader needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26.2 or later.

1. [Download]({{ site.repo_url }}/releases/tag/v0.0.1) the zip (459 MB). It appears in your **Downloads** folder.
2. Double-click the zip, then drag **Cloudy Reader** to **Applications**. Cloudy Reader is in the Applications folder.
3. Open Cloudy Reader and click **Done**, not the Trash button, when macOS [blocks the first open]({{ '/manual/getting-started/#open-the-app-for-the-first-time' | relative_url }}). In **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway**, then open the app again and click **Open** to see the window.
4. Keep the window open and the Mac online while the voices download, about 0.66 GB. In about 1 minute, the **Voice** menu shows a voice, and **Speak** is no longer dimmed. If no voice appears, see [No voice is installed]({{ '/manual/troubleshooting/#the-status-bar-says-no-voice-is-installed' | relative_url }}).
5. Paste any question, or the sample on [Get started]({{ '/manual/getting-started/#read-your-first-question' | relative_url }}), and press Command-Return. The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s.", and you hear the question.
