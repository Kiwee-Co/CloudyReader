Cloudy Reader needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26.2 or later.

1. [Download the zip]({{ site.repo_url }}/releases/tag/v0.0.1) (459 MB).
2. Double-click the zip, then drag **Cloudy Reader** to **Applications** before you open it. The app is in the **Applications** folder.
3. Open Cloudy Reader. When macOS [blocks the first launch]({{ '/manual/getting-started/#open-the-app-for-the-first-time' | relative_url }}), close the warning dialog, and do not move the app to the Trash. In **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway**, open the app again, and click **Open**. The window opens.
4. Keep the window open and the Mac online while the voices download, about 0.66 GB. The first voice usually arrives in under a minute. The **Voice** menu then shows it, and **Speak** is no longer dimmed. If no voice arrives, see [No voice is installed]({{ '/manual/troubleshooting/#the-status-bar-says-no-voice-is-installed' | relative_url }}).
5. Copy any question, or the sample on [Get started]({{ '/manual/getting-started/#read-your-first-question' | relative_url }}). Click the question box, press Command-V, then press Command-Return. The status bar shows "Preparing…", then "Playing _n_ s.", and you hear the question.

[Get started]({{ '/manual/getting-started/' | relative_url }}) explains each step.
